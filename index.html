Frequently asked questions
A few things to know before you start.

        

          
Is ClipText free?
ClipText is being built around a free-to-use experience. Hosting and processing options will be kept within free service limits wherever possible.

          
Do I need to create an account?
No account is needed to use this simple interface.

          
Can I transcribe any TikTok video?
Not every video can be accessed. Availability can depend on the video's privacy settings, region, and TikTok's access restrictions.

          
Which languages are supported?
Language support depends on the transcript source and processing engine. We will publish confirmed language support after testing.

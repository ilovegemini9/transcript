Frequently asked questions
A few things to know before you start.

        

          
Is ClipText free?
ClipText is free to try and does not require an account. Transcript processing depends on provider capacity and usage limits, which may change.

          
Do I need to create an account?
No account is needed to use this simple interface.

          
Can I transcribe any TikTok video?
No. The video must be public and accessible. Private, deleted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable videos may not be processed.

          
Does ClipText work when a video has no captions?
ClipText requests automatic speech-recognition fallback when supported by the transcript provider. Success depends on video access, audio quality, language, and provider availability.

What is a video transcript?

          
A searchable text version of the spoken words in a video from TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram.

        

        

          
Turn video speech into text
A video transcript makes spoken information easier to read, search, quote, study, and organize. Paste one public video link into the tool above to request its transcript.

          
Use transcripts responsibly
Check the text against the original video before sharing it. Speech recognition can mishear names, accents, background audio, and technical terms. Only process videos you are allowed to use.

What to expect from a video transcript

          
A practical guide to getting readable text from a public video.

        

        

          

            
Captions and automatic speech recognition

            
Some videos already have captions or subtitle tracks. When available to the transcript provider, these can be used as a source for the text. If captions are not available, automatic speech recognition may be used when supported. Availability varies by video and provider, so a transcript is not guaranteed for every link.

          

          

            
What affects transcript accuracy?

            
Clear speech usually produces more useful text. Background music, overlapping speakers, accents, fast speech, specialist terms, and noisy recordings can cause mistakes. Review names, dates, figures, and quotations against the original video before relying on them.

          

          

            
Use and accessibility

            
Readable text can help you search spoken information, make personal study notes, review a tutorial, or follow content when replaying audio is inconvenient. A generated transcript is an aid, not a verified or official record of what a speaker said.

          

          

            
Public links and privacy

            
Only submit videos you are allowed to process. The tool does not bypass private-account settings, logins, age restrictions, or regional restrictions. A public URL may still be inaccessible to the provider, and you should keep your own copy of any transcript you need.

Frequently asked questions
A few things to know before you start.

        

          
Is Transcript TikTok free?
Transcript TikTok is free to try and does not require an account. Transcript processing depends on provider capacity and usage limits, which may change.

          
Do I need to create an account?
No account is needed to use this simple interface.

          
Can I transcribe any video?
No. The video must be public and accessible. Private, deleted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable videos may not be processed.

          
Does Transcript TikTok work when a video has no captions?
Transcript TikTok requests automatic speech-recognition fallback when supported by the transcript provider. Success depends on video access, audio quality, language, and provider availability.

          
Which video platforms are supported?
The tool accepts accessible public video links from TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram. Private or restricted videos may not work.

          
Can I create summaries, captions, and hooks?
Optional AI tools can summarize a transcript and suggest social captions or video hooks when the site owner has configured an AI provider. Free-tier usage limits may apply.

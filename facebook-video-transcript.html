Facebook Video Transcript

Extract spoken words from an accessible public Facebook video and turn them into text you can review, copy, and save. Paste a Facebook video or fb.watch link.

How to get a Facebook transcript

  1. Open a public Facebook video and use its Share option.
  2. Copy the video link, including a supported fb.watch short link.
  3. Paste it above, generate the transcript, and check the text.

Why get a Facebook transcript?

A Facebook video transcript can make a public interview, community update, event clip, or explainer easier to search and review. You can save the text as notes instead of relying on memory after watching.

Tips for a more useful transcript

Open the individual video first, then use Facebook’s Share option to copy its link; supported fb.watch links can also be pasted. A post being visible in your feed does not mean an external transcript service can retrieve it. If processing fails, check that the video opens publicly in a browser without your personal login and that the link points to the video rather than only to a post or profile.

Supported links and limitations

Use a public link that the source platform and transcript provider can access. Private, deleted, age-restricted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable videos may not work. We do not bypass privacy settings. Automatic speech recognition can make mistakes, especially with names, numbers, accents, slang, and background music. Check important passages against the original video.

Privacy and control

Submit one video at a time. Do not paste private links or content you do not have permission to process. Keep a copy of important transcripts yourself; the tool does not promise cloud storage or permanent transcript retention.

Frequently asked questions

Which Facebook links are supported?

Public facebook.com video links and supported fb.watch links can be submitted. Private or login-restricted content may not be accessible.

Can I transcribe a private Facebook video?

No. The tool does not bypass privacy settings or account restrictions. Only videos accessible to the transcript provider can be processed.

Can I save the result?

When a transcript is returned, you can copy the text or download it as a TXT file.

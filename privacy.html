Privacy Policy

This page explains how Transcript TikTok handles information submitted through its public video transcript tools. The service accepts accessible public video links from TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

Information submitted

When you request a transcript, the video URL is sent from our server to our transcript provider, Supadata, to retrieve or generate the transcript. The transcript response is returned to your browser for display. The provider may process the submitted URL and related video data under its own privacy terms and technical retention policies.

AI features and third-party AI providers

If AI repurposing features are enabled and you request a summary, captions, or hooks, the transcript text you submit is sent from our server to the configured AI provider. Depending on server configuration, this may be OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or OpenAI. Those providers process submitted text under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit confidential or sensitive transcript content. AI features may be disabled and are not required for basic transcription.

Accounts and saved transcripts

The basic transcript workflow does not require an account. Transcript TikTok does not currently provide a cloud transcript library or cross-device sync. Download any transcript you want to keep. Do not assume that results are stored for later retrieval.

Private and sensitive content

Only submit public links and content you are authorized to process. Do not submit private, confidential, sensitive, or unauthorized material. The tool does not bypass platform privacy settings or access restrictions.

Service providers and availability

Transcript processing relies on a third-party provider. Its availability, processing, and data handling may change. Transcript TikTok does not control the provider's systems or promise a particular retention period.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit a URL and can download the transcript if you want to keep a personal copy. For questions about this policy, contact the project owner through the public project repository.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its providers change. The latest version will be published on this page.

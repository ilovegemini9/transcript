Privacy Policy

ClipText processes public video links submitted by users to return transcript text. This page is a plain-language summary for the current prototype.

What we process

We process the URL you submit and the transcript response needed to complete your request. The browser workspace stores saved links locally in your browser. We do not ask for an account on the public transcript page.

Temporary processing

Transcript processing may use a third-party provider. Do not submit private, confidential, or unauthorized content. Provider availability and retention may vary, so the service should be used for public videos you are allowed to process.

Your choices

You can clear local workspace data from the Workspace page. For questions or deletion requests, contact the ClipText owner through the project repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated as accounts, cloud history, billing, or additional platforms are introduced.

TikTok Transcript Generator

Convert the spoken words in a public TikTok video into text you can search, review, copy, and save. Paste one supported TikTok link to get started.

How to get a TikTok transcript

  1. Open a public TikTok video and tap Share.
  2. Choose Copy link, then paste the link above.
  3. Select Get transcript. Review the result before using or quoting it.

Why convert video to text?

A TikTok transcript can help you revisit a tutorial, capture an idea, search spoken information, or prepare notes from a public video.

Supported links and limitations

Use a public link that the source platform and transcript provider can access. Private, deleted, age-restricted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable videos may not work. We do not bypass privacy settings. Automatic speech recognition can make mistakes, especially with names, numbers, accents, slang, and background music. Check important passages against the original video.

Privacy and control

Submit one video at a time. Do not paste private links or content you do not have permission to process. Keep a copy of important transcripts yourself; the tool does not promise cloud storage or permanent transcript retention.

Frequently asked questions

Which TikTok links work?

Use a public TikTok video link, including supported vm.tiktok.com short links. Private, deleted, or restricted videos may not be accessible.

Can I download the transcript?

Yes. When text is returned, use Copy text or Download TXT to save it.

Is the transcript always exact?

No. Automatic speech recognition can miss names, numbers, slang, and words under music. Check important passages against the original video.

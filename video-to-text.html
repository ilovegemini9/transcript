How to get a video transcript

  1. Copy a public video link from TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram.
  2. Paste the link into the box above.

Why get a video transcript?

A video transcript turns spoken content into text you can search, review, copy, and keep. Use it to make study notes, revisit instructions, research a public interview, or organize ideas from a tutorial across supported platforms.

Tips for a more useful transcript

Start with a public TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram video link. This tool handles one link per request; choose a video with clear speech and check the transcript before relying on names, figures, or quotations.

Supported links and limitations

Use a public link that the source platform and transcript provider can access. Private, deleted, age-restricted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable videos may not work. We do not bypass privacy settings. Automatic speech recognition can make mistakes, especially with names, numbers, accents, slang, and background music. Check important passages against the original video.

Privacy and control

Submit one video at a time. Do not paste private links or content you do not have permission to process. Keep a copy of important transcripts yourself; the tool does not promise cloud storage or permanent transcript retention.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms can I use?

The tool currently accepts supported public TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram links.

Can I submit multiple videos at once?

No. The workflow processes one video link per request so you can review each transcript separately.

Are all videos guaranteed to work?

No. Private, deleted, restricted, or inaccessible videos may fail. Processing and transcript quality depend on the source video and provider availability.
